Duck Breasts with Honey

Ingredients:
  • 2 duck breasts
  • 6 shallots
  • 10 cl balsamic vinegar
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • Salt, pepper
Preparation:

Prepare the duck breasts: remove excess fat but keep the skin. Make criss-cross cuts in the skin down to the flesh. Season with salt and pepper.

Heat a non-stick pan without fat. Place the breasts skin side down. Cook 5–10 minutes, basting with the rendered fat. Remove the breasts. Drain excess fat and return the duck to the pan, flesh side down, and cook about 5 more minutes. Remove and keep warm.

In the same pan, sauté peeled and chopped shallots. Deglaze with balsamic vinegar, scrape up the cooking juices, add honey and bring to a boil for 1 minute. Slice the duck and coat with the sauce. Serve with fresh pasta.

Pepperoni Pizza

Ingredients:
  • 150 g pepperoni slices
  • 2 Mozzarella di Bufala
  • 200 g fresh tomato purée
  • 1 white onion
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • ½ bunch basil
  • 1 pizza dough
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
Preparation:

Preheat the oven to 250°C.

Sauté the onion and garlic in olive oil, season, then put into a blender.

Add the tomato purée and some basil leaves. Blend until smooth.

Drain the mozzarella and slice it.

Roll out the dough on a baking tray, spread a thin layer of tomato sauce and place mozzarella slices.

Bake for 8 minutes. Remove, add pepperoni slices, then bake 2 more minutes.

Finish with fresh basil and serve hot.

Apple Tart

Ingredients:
  • 250 g flour
  • 125 g butter
  • 1 egg
  • 3 large organic apples
  • 100 g almond powder
  • Maple syrup
Preparation:

Make the dough in a stand mixer with flour, sugar, melted butter and egg.

Let rest 1 hour then roll out and place in a tart tin on parchment paper.

Spread almond powder on the tart base and arrange apple slices on top.

Bake 30 minutes at 180°C. Glaze with a little maple syrup and bake 5 minutes more.

Deviled Eggs

Ingredients:
  • 6 eggs
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp mustard
  • Salt, pepper
  • Chives for garnish
Preparation:

Boil eggs for 10 minutes. Cool, peel and cut in half.

Remove yolks and mash with mayonnaise, mustard, salt and pepper.

Fill whites with the mixture and garnish with chives. Serve chilled.

Caesar Salad

Ingredients:
  • 1 romaine lettuce heart
  • 150 g chicken fillets (or croutons)
  • Parmesan shavings
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 garlic clove
  • Salt, pepper
Preparation:

Grill chicken fillets and slice. Wash and dry romaine lettuce.

Make the dressing by mixing mayonnaise, lemon juice, pressed garlic, salt and pepper.

Toss lettuce with chicken, parmesan and dressing. Add croutons if desired.

Chocolate Mousse

Ingredients:
  • 200 g dark chocolate
  • 4 eggs (separate whites and yolks)
  • 30 g sugar (optional)
  • A pinch of salt
Preparation:

Melt chocolate in a bain-marie and let cool slightly. Mix egg yolks into melted chocolate.

Whisk egg whites with a pinch of salt until stiff and gently fold into chocolate mixture.

Divide into dishes and chill at least 2 hours before serving.
